## Q3: WHAT CAN AN AGENT DO THAT THEY CAN'T?

### Their product

A workspace where YOU draft, review, and sign.
AI assists while you work.

**Example:**
ContractAI shows "⚠ High Risk: §4.2 Unlimited liability"
You decide what to do.

### Your agent

Holds a goal across 20 steps.
Re-reads state that changed.
Decides autonomously.

**Example:**
You say: "Review our contracts and find liability risks."
Agent: "Found 3 contracts with unlimited liability.
        Compared against playbook.
        Drafted capped versions.
        Queued for your approval."
