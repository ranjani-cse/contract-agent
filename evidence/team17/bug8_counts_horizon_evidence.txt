Bug 8 evidence: renewal_forecast counts.in_horizon varies with limit

{horizon_days: 60, limit: 20}   -> rows=16, counts={'in_horizon': 16, ...}
{horizon_days: 60, limit: 100}  -> rows=78, counts={'in_horizon': 78, ...}
{horizon_days: 60}              -> rows=16, counts={'in_horizon': 16, ...}
{limit: 100}                    -> rows=83, counts={'in_horizon': 83, ...}
{}                              -> rows=16, counts={'in_horizon': 16, ...}

The 60-day horizon is a property of the data. counts.in_horizon should be the same regardless of limit. Instead limit acts as a scan window: the tool scans the first `limit` contracts and reports how many fall in the horizon, presenting the result as a total.

Schema description: "Board mode: contracts scanned per page (clamped to 1..scan limit, default 20)."

Repro: python3 -c "

    print(args, 'rows=', len(data.get('contracts', [])), 'in_horizon=', data.get('counts', {}).get('in_horizon'))
