## Product Overview

Gerri is an AI contract review agent that automates redlining for teams who need to close contracts faster while controlling risk. It uses playbook-based review and counter-proposal generation to speed up the contract review process.

---

## Core Features

### 1. Smart Playbooks
- Give Gerri historical contracts and decisions
- Gerri builds your first playbook automatically
- Playbooks become institutional memory
- Gets smarter with every new contract

### 2. Proactive Recommendation
- Gerri reviews past contracts and customer pushback
- Identifies areas to change before future redlines happen
- Prevents problems instead of reacting

### 3. Custom Instructions
- Define risk posture once
- Gerri applies it across every contract
- Security, finance, and legal define rules for their area

### 4. Auto-Review
- Drop in counterparty redlines
- Gerri reviews against your playbook in minutes
- Flags where terms diverge
- Checks if risk fits your tolerance

### 5. Counter Proposal Generation
- Gerri suggests acceptable counter-language
- Inserts it directly into the doc
- Send same day, not same week

### 6. Gmail Integration
- Gerri reviews a contract the moment it hits your inbox

### 7. Smart Escalation
- Routes out-of-playbook items to the right owner
- Legal, security, sales, or finance
- Ensures the right party handles edge cases

### 8. Approval Workflow
- Human checkpoint to accept, reject, or override Gerri direction
- At each checkpoint:
  - Make it a rule moving forward, OR
  - Treat it as a one-time exception
- This is how the playbook learns

### 9. Audit History
- Full record of:
  - Every decision
  - Every rule applied
  - Every rationale
- Builds trust in the automated process
