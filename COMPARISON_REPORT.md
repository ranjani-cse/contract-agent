---

## 5. KEY INSIGHT

Their products are AI-assisted. Our agent is AI-autonomous.

Gerri is the closest competitor. It proves the market wants playbook-based review with counter-proposals. But Gerri still requires you to upload the contract, choose when to review, and drive each step.

Our agent does the same work, but finds the contracts, decides what to review, and acts on its own.

---

## 6. CONCLUSION

| Product | Strength | Weakness |
|---------|----------|----------|
| Ironclad | Mature execution | Weak AI |
| ContractAI | AI-native, full lifecycle | AI assists, not autonomous |
| Gerri | Closest to our agent | Human drives every step |
| Our Agent | Autonomous, portfolio-wide | No UI, no templates |

The gap is not features. It is autonomy.
